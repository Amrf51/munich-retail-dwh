## Internal arithmetic consistency

Do the money columns agree with each other? This decides which fields can be trusted and which have to be recomputed.

| Identity | Holds | Testable | Share |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `unit_price = base_price x (1 - discount_rate)` | 35,491 | 35,491 | 100.0% |
| `total_amount = quantity x unit_price` | 34,252 | 35,268 | 97.1% |
| `tax_amount = total_amount x 0.19` | 34,515 | 35,510 | 97.2% |

The **1,016** total mismatches by status: Completed **963**, Returned **28**, Canceled **25**

> The price identity holds universally while the total identity does not, so the price side is trustworthy and `total_amount` is the corrupt field.
